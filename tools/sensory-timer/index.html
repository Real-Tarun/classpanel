Guided Breathing Rhythms for Meditation, Stress Relief & Focus

Whether you are taking a 3-minute mindfulness break between work calls or helping students settle down after recess, rhythmic breathing synchronizes your heart rate variability (HRV) and soothes the central nervous system:

Scientific Breathing Patterns Explained

  • Box Breathing (4-4-4-4): Used by Navy SEALs, athletes, and meditators for instant situational composure under pressure. 4s Inhale, 4s Hold, 4s Exhale, 4s Hold.
  • 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath: Dr. Andrew Weil's natural tranquilizer technique. A prolonged 8-second exhale activates the vagus nerve to slow racing heartbeats.
  • 5-5 Heart Coherence: Balances sympathetic (fight or flight) and parasympathetic (rest and digest) nervous states for calm mental clarity.
  • Sensory Calming Liquid Droplets: Non-demanding visual focus designed for classrooms, neurodivergent students, quiet rooms, and sensory decompression corners.
