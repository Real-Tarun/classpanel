How to Use the Talking Clock

The large clock face shows the current time updating every second. Click "Speak Time Now" to have your browser read the current time aloud using built-in text-to-speech — no external service, no cost, no microphone needed. Set the auto-announce interval to hear the time spoken automatically every 1, 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes — useful for time awareness in classrooms or as an accessibility aid.

  • Classroom Time Awareness: Young students hear the time announced without constantly asking.
  • Accessibility Aid: Ideal for visually impaired users who need audible time updates.
  • Study Timer: Hear time announcements in background while staying focused on work.
