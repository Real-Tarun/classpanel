Standardized Examination Timing for Certifications, Universities & Schools

Administering exams demands absolute clarity, strict silence, and total transparency. ClassPanel's Official Exam Timer provides proctors, invigilators, students, and self-study candidates with an authoritative, distraction-free board.

Test Center & Self-Study Features

  • Synchronized Live Wall Clock: Displays exact local real time alongside remaining exam countdown so candidates can accurately calculate time per section.
  • Dedicated Reading Period: Configure an automated pre-exam reading period (5m, 10m, 15m) that automatically rolls into writing time with visual status transitions.
  • Silent Visual Milestones: Avoids startling candidates with shrill audio alerts during quiet test halls while flashing clear banner reminders at key intervals (Halfway, 15 Min, 5 Min).

Standardized Test Practice (SAT, GRE, MCAT, AWS)

Simulate realistic test day conditions at home. Configure the exact time limit for each test section, project the screen, and practice pacing under genuine exam pressure.
