How to Use the Random Group Generator

Paste your classroom roster or participant names into the roster box, with one name per line. Next, choose your group formation method: either by "Number of Groups" (e.g. partition the class into exactly 6 lab pods) or by "Group Size" (e.g. create teams of 3 or 4 students each). Click the "Generate Groups" button to instantly sort participants into balanced, color-coded team cards.

If you need to make tactical adjustments—such as separating two students who should not work together—simply drag and drop student name tags between group cards. You can assign unique names to each team (e.g. Team Falcon, Team Phoenix), shuffle teams again with one click, or export the completed team roster for seating charts and grading sheets.

Why Use ClassPanel's Fair Group Generator?

Allowing students to choose their own partners often reinforces exclusive social cliques, leaves timid students feeling socially isolated, and consumes ten minutes of instructional time. ClassPanel creates balanced, impartial teams in seconds, eliminating social anxiety and encouraging cross-classroom collaboration.

In strict compliance with student privacy laws (COPPA and FERPA), ClassPanel stores all student roster data locally in your browser cache. No names are ever uploaded to cloud databases or shared with external analytics services, guaranteeing complete confidentiality for school districts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create teams by group count or members per group?

Yes. Choose either "Number of Groups" (e.g. split into 4 equal teams) or "Group Size" (e.g. exactly 3 students per team) to suit your activity.

Can I manually move students between groups after generating?

Yes, our drag-and-drop interface lets you easily swap or rebalance students between generated team cards.

Does the group maker work offline in classrooms without Wi-Fi?

Yes, once loaded, the team generation algorithms run entirely offline inside your browser without an active connection.
