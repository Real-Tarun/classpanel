Instant, Fair Group Formation for Workplaces, Sports & Classrooms

Forming groups manually can consume valuable time and inadvertently create social cliques. ClassPanel's Random Group Generator creates balanced, unbiased groups with one click for any meeting, project, or sport:

Workshops, Agile Teams & Hackathons

Form rapid breakout teams for brainstorming sprints, pair-programming sessions, or company offsite icebreakers. Click "Copy Groups" to paste the roster distribution directly into Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Notion.

Sports Tournaments & Recreation Leagues
