How to Use the Classroom & Study Timer

Select your countdown duration by clicking any of the quick preset chips—such as one minute, five minutes, ten minutes, fifteen minutes, thirty minutes, or one hour. If you require a specific interval, click the "⏱️ Custom" chip to type exact hours, minutes, and seconds. Once your time is set, click the giant green "Start" button to launch the countdown. You can pause at any time, tap "Reset" to return to your chosen duration, or use the quick increment buttons (+1 Hr, +5 Min, +1 Min, -1 Min) to adjust time on the fly without stopping your lesson.

To maximize classroom visibility, click the Fullscreen button in the header bar or press the keyboard shortcut to expand the timer across your entire smartboard or projector screen. Under the audio control panel, choose an optional ambient focus soundtrack (such as calm meditation, rain sounds, or alpha wave beats) to mask hallway chatter during independent work. You can also select your preferred end-of-timer alarm chime, ranging from gentle marimba chimes to cheerful school bells.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this classroom timer offline on my smartboard?

Yes! ClassPanel is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded in your browser, the timer, ambient focus soundscapes, and progress ring animations function 100% offline without requiring internet access.

Does the timer support keyboard shortcuts for smartboard clickers?

Yes. Press the Spacebar to Start or Pause the countdown, press R to Reset to your preset duration, and press F or the Projector button to toggle Fullscreen projector mode.

Is this timer free for public schools and COPPA/FERPA compliant?

Absolutely. ClassPanel is completely free with no student account registration, no passwords, no ads during countdown, and zero personal data tracking, making it fully compliant with student privacy laws.

How does Pomodoro mode work in the classroom?

Click the Pomodoro chip to run 25-minute focused work blocks followed by 5-minute restorative brain breaks. It helps students maintain high concentration during independent study and essay writing.
