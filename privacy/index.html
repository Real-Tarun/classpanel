Privacy Policy & Student Data Protection

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

1. The Short & Simple Truth

ClassPanel.online does not collect, sell, store, or transmit any student personal information. Period.

2. COPPA & FERPA Compliance

We are fully dedicated to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA):

  • No Student Accounts: Students and teachers never need to register, log in, or provide email addresses.
  • Local Browser Storage Only: Any student names you paste into the Random Name Picker or Group Generator exist solely in your browser's local sandbox (`localStorage`). It never leaves your computer.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site behavioral tracking beacons.

3. Data Deletion

Teachers can erase all locally stored rosters, saved presets, and custom timer configurations at any time simply by clicking "Delete Roster" within the tools or by clearing their browser's cache and local storage.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or school district privacy inquiry questions, please reach out to us via our Contact Page.
