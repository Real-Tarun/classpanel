How to Use the Sensory & Meditation Timer

Select your preferred visual relaxation mode from the top theme tabs: Liquid Lava Drip, Soothing Sand Hourglass, or Guided Breathwork. Set your target mindfulness duration using the quick presets (1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, 10 minutes) or enter a custom duration. Click "Begin" to start the fluid visual motion, which flows continuously as the gentle countdown proceeds.

When using the guided breathwork mode, select your preferred breathing rhythm: Box Breathing (4-4-4-4 inhale, hold, exhale, hold) for focus, or 4-7-8 Calm for deep relaxation. An expanding and contracting visual orb guides students through each breath cycle in synchronization with soft acoustic Tibetan singing bowl tones, helping regulate heart rates and calm the room.

Why Use Sensory Timers with Younger & Neurodiverse Students?

Loud electronic buzzers and aggressive red countdown digits often trigger fight-or-flight anxiety in sensory-sensitive children and students with ADHD or autism spectrum differences. Sensory timers replace digital urgency with hypnotic, calming physical simulations that ground classroom energy and ease difficult transitions without stress.

Whether helping children settle down after energetic playground recess, calming pre-exam jitters before standardized testing, or providing a quiet anchor in a classroom calm-down corner, ClassPanel's sensory tools foster emotional self-regulation in a completely ad-free, private environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What guided breathing rhythms are available?

Choose between Box Breathing (4-4-4-4 for focus), 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath (for anxiety relief), and Coherence Breathing (5.5s cycles for emotional regulation).

Is this sensory timer helpful for ADHD and neurodivergent students?

Yes. The gentle, fluid animations and peaceful colors provide a soothing visual focal point that assists sensory regulation and smooth transitions without sudden alarms.
