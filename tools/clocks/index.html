Teaching Time on Interactive Smartboards

Learning how to read analog clock faces is a core elementary mathematics standard. ClassPanel's Classroom Clocks bridges the gap between traditional clock hands and digital time displays.

Time-Telling Quiz Mode

Click "Hide Digital (Quiz Mode)" to conceal the digital readout. Point to the analog hands on your projector or smartboard and ask students: "What time is it?" Once students answer, click to reveal the digital time and verify accuracy.
