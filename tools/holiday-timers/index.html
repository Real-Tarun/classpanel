How to Use the Date Countdown & Holiday Timer

Begin by choosing an upcoming holiday or school milestone from the curated presets—including Summer Vacation, Winter Break, Halloween, Thanksgiving, New Year's Day, and the 100th Day of School. To track a personal milestone, wedding, sports tournament, or district testing date, select "Custom Event" and pick your exact target date and time using the calendar picker.

Once active, the dashboard calculates the exact duration remaining down to days, hours, minutes, and seconds. Click "Live Stream Mode" to launch a distraction-free, cinematic high-contrast display suitable for projection in school hallways, auditorium screens, or live video broadcasts. You can also copy a shareable link that encodes your custom deadline directly into the URL for students and colleagues.

Why Use ClassPanel's Event & Holiday Countdown?

Long-term planning and calendar math are essential developmental skills for students. Having an aesthetic, synchronized countdown clock projected during homeroom or morning circle fosters positive anticipation, helps students contextualize seasonal changes, and builds excitement for upcoming academic breaks and celebrations.

ClassPanel's date countdown runs entirely in your local browser without third-party tracking cookies or registration walls. It supports both light and dark display modes, calculates timezones accurately, and allows schools to maintain an elegant digital ticker on cafeteria monitors and library kiosks completely free of charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I countdown to custom dates and holidays?

Yes, select upcoming holidays (Christmas, New Year, Halloween, Summer Break) or enter any future target date and time.

Can I use this countdown in OBS live streams?

Yes, copy the URL into your OBS browser source for a clean, distraction-free countdown overlay.
