How to Use the Split / Lap Timer

Press the prominent "Start" button or press the spacebar to initiate precision timing. As an athletic lap, science laboratory trial, reading interval, or segmented classroom activity progresses, click the "Lap (Split)" button at the conclusion of each distinct phase. The timer instantly records that segment's elapsed duration in the split log while the master clock continues running uninterrupted in the background.

Review your segment split times in the structured data table below, which automatically calculates the delta (+/-) compared to your preceding lap. The table highlights your fastest split interval with an emerald medal badge and your slowest split interval with an alert badge, allowing instant pace comparison. You can pause, resume, or reset the board at any time with dedicated action buttons.
