How to Use the Chess Clock & Timer

Configure your match time controls by picking from standard competitive presets—including Bullet (1 min), Blitz (3 min or 5 min), Rapid (10 min or 15 min), or Classical (30 min). You can also add an optional Fischer increment (seconds added per move, such as 3+2 or 5+3) for formal competitive play.

Once the match starts, Player 1's clock ticks down. As soon as a move is completed on the physical board, the player taps their screen side or hits the spacebar to pause their clock and instantly activate the opponent's timer. High-contrast colors and time-out alerts signal clearly when a flag falls.

Why Use ClassPanel's Chess Clock for School Clubs & Tournaments?

Dedicated digital chess clocks are costly, fragile, and frequently run out of specialized batteries right before school club tournaments. ClassPanel turns any shared tablet, Chromebook, or smartphone into a dual-player FIDE-style chess clock in seconds.

The responsive two-panel touchscreen design accommodates players sitting across from one another at a desk, featuring zero advertisements, intuitive tap targets, and smooth Web Audio click feedback for fair, competitive chess play.

Frequently Asked Questions

What tournament time controls are included?

Features Bullet (1m), Blitz (3m, 5m), Rapid (10m, 15m), and Classical presets with customizable Fischer increment seconds (+1s, +2s, +5s).

Can I switch turns using the keyboard?

Yes, press the Spacebar to alternate turns or click anywhere on the active clock side.
