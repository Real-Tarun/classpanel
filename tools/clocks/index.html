Aesthetic Fullscreen Online Clock for Study Desks, Workspaces & Classrooms

From maintaining focus during study sessions to teaching elementary students how to tell time, having a clean, prominent clock keeps you anchored to your schedule:

Desk & Study Monitor Display

Use Projector Mode (F11) to turn an extra tablet, iPad, or secondary monitor into an elegant desktop clock. The high-contrast numerals and smooth second-hand motion give an instant, glanceable read without screen clutter.

Time-Telling Practice & Quiz Mode

Click "Hide Digital (Quiz Mode)" to conceal the digital readout. Point to the analog hands on your screen and ask: "What time is it?" Once an answer is given, click to reveal the digital time and verify accuracy.
