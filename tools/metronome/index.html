How to Use the Online Metronome

Set your desired tempo using the intuitive BPM slider, or fine-tune beat precision using the +1 and -1 increment buttons across an extensive range of 40 to 208 beats per minute. If you have a specific song rhythm in mind, tap the "Tap Tempo" button in time with your music to calculate the BPM automatically.

Select your target time signature (4/4, 3/4, 2/4, or 6/8) using the preset chips. The metronome indicates the primary downbeat with a distinct high-pitched acoustic accent and animated visual indicator pulse, followed by softer secondary beats to maintain musical structure during practice.

Why Choose ClassPanel's Online Metronome for Music Practice?

Musicians, music teachers, and student band performers require dependable, sample-accurate rhythmic timing that never stutters or lags. ClassPanel uses the browser's native Web Audio API to schedule beat clicks ahead of time on the audio hardware thread, ensuring flawless rhythmic accuracy.

With zero intrusive advertisements to disrupt rehearsal, an easy-to-read full-screen beat pendulum, and full offline PWA support, this metronome is the ideal practice companion for instrumentalists, choir directors, and music classrooms.
