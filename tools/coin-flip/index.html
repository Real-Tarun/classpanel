The Mathematics and Utility of Coin Flipping

Flipping a coin has been the universal method for resolving binary decisions for millennia. Known as navia aut caput ("ship or head") in Ancient Rome, modern coin tosses serve critical functions across sports, game theory, and classrooms:

  • 50/50 Theoretical Probability: Over large sample sizes, a fair coin converges on an exact 50% probability distribution (the Law of Large Numbers).
  • Sports & Officiating: Used worldwide in football, soccer, cricket, and tennis to impartially determine starting kickoff, ball possession, and end selection.
  • Multi-Coin Probability Experiments: Flipping 2, 3, or 5 coins simultaneously demonstrates binomial distributions (such as the 1:2:1 ratio for 2 coins: HH, HT/TH, TT).
  • Cryptographic Randomness: Our coin toss uses browser cryptographically secure random values (`crypto.getRandomValues`) to eliminate mechanical bias.
