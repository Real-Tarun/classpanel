Supporting Classroom Mindfulness & Emotional Regulation

Classrooms are vibrant and energetic, but overstimulation can lead to fatigue, sensory overload, and loss of focus. Sensory timers provide visual pacing that guides students toward autonomic calming.

Box Breathing Technique (4-4-4-4)

Used by athletes and wellness professionals, box breathing triggers the parasympathetic nervous system: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold for 4 seconds. Our animated breathing orb gives students an intuitive focal point.

Calm-Down Corner Support

Place a tablet or Chromebook in your classroom calm-down corner running the liquid bubble timer. Watching the slow descent of droplets helps dysregulated students regain composure without feeling punished.
