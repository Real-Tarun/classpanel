How to Use the Random Number Generator

Specify your desired numerical range by typing the minimum and maximum boundaries into the Min and Max input boxes (e.g. 1 to 100 for percentage raffles, or 1 to 30 for student seat numbers). Next, choose how many random numbers you want to generate in a single draw. If you are conducting a ticket raffle or door prize where numbers cannot be drawn twice, check the "Unique Only (No Repeats)" box.

Click the "Generate Numbers" button to trigger the instant randomization roll. The results appear in large, high-visibility number tiles, with newly drawn numbers added to the chronological history log below. You can sort results in ascending or descending order, copy all generated numbers with one click, or export the log for classroom record-keeping.

Why Choose ClassPanel's Random Number Generator?

From selecting raffle winners and assigning lockers to running math probability experiments and spontaneous arithmetic drills, random number generation is a daily classroom essential. ClassPanel utilizes the browser's native cryptographic random number engine (crypto.getRandomValues), ensuring statistically unbiased, uniform distribution for all numerical queries.

The interface is intentionally free of clutter, flashy promotional banners, and tracking scripts that slow down older school hardware. It works offline as a progressive web app, provides instantaneous copying, and remembers your custom range settings locally without requiring an account or subscription.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I generate numbers without duplicate repetitions?

Yes, toggle "No Duplicates" when generating batches of numbers for lotteries, classroom seat assignments, or contest draws.

What is the maximum number range supported?

You can choose any custom range from negative numbers up to millions with cryptographic randomness.
