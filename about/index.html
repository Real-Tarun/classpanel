ClassPanel / About

Designed by Teachers, Built for the Classroom

ClassPanel was born out of everyday teacher frustration. Every day, educators across the world project online countdown timers, student pickers, and dice onto classroom smartboards — only to be bombarded with flashing banner ads, intrusive video commercials, cookie consent banners, and slow load times.

Our Core Commitments to Educators

  • 100% Free Forever: No premium tiers, no paywalls, and no subscription traps. ClassPanel is freely available to any school, teacher, or student worldwide.
  • Zero Ads & Pure Focus: No third-party ad networks, popups, or tracking scripts that disrupt lessons or distract students.
  • Complete Student Privacy (COPPA & FERPA): We collect zero personal student data. All student names and class rosters are stored strictly inside your local browser.
  • 100% Offline Progressive Web App (PWA): When school Wi-Fi drops out, your timers and tools continue working seamlessly without internet connectivity.
