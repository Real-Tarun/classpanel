How to Use the Presentation & Toastmasters Timer

Select your presentation format from the standard speech presets—including 1-minute elevator pitches, 2-minute lightning talks, 5-minute project overviews, 7-minute Toastmasters speeches, or full 20-minute keynote lectures. Alternatively, enter custom warning thresholds for Green (body phase), Amber (wind-down warning), and Red (hard conclusion).

Click "Start" to launch the timer. The display remains a calm, neutral color until the Green threshold is reached, confirming the minimum qualifying speech length. When the Amber threshold arrives, the screen shifts to cautionary amber signaling the speaker to summarize. At the Red threshold, the display turns vibrant red indicating time has expired, with an optional gentle acoustic chime notifying the room.

Why Choose ClassPanel's Speech & Presentation Timer?

Effective public speaking and debate skills require strict respect for time limits. Without objective visual cues, student speakers either rush through their arguments or run over allotted time, throwing conference schedules and classroom periods into chaos. ClassPanel provides the internationally recognized Toastmasters traffic light signals in high-contrast visibility.

The interface is designed for discrete stage placement. It can be positioned on a podium tablet or projected across a lecture hall without intrusive advertisements or distracting controls, allowing speakers and proctors to stay fully focused on communication delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the traffic light signals work?

The screen illuminates Green when minimum qualifying time is reached, Amber at the warning benchmark, and Red when maximum allotted time has expired.

Can I customize the green, yellow, and red intervals?

Yes, select common speech formats (such as 1-2 min Table Topics or 5-7 min Prepared Speeches) or input custom minute benchmarks.
