How to Play Rock Paper Scissors Online & Winning Strategies

Rock Paper Scissors (also known as Roshambo or Jan-Ken-Pon) is one of the world's most widely recognized zero-sum hand games. While fundamentally a game of chance, psychological research has uncovered predictable human patterns:

  • The Rules: Rock crushes Scissors, Scissors cuts Paper, and Paper covers Rock. Identical choices result in a tie.
  • Winner's Bias: Human players who win a round tend to repeat the same winning move, while losers instinctively switch to the move that would have beaten the previous winner.
  • Best of 3/5/7 Series: Use tournament series mode to settle disputes, determine classroom turns, pick draft order in fantasy leagues, or make fair daily choices.
