How to Use the Online Tally Counter

Click the large "+1" button or press the spacebar / up-arrow on your keyboard to increment the count by one. To decrement, click "-1" or press the down-arrow. You can customize the step value (e.g. count by 2s, 5s, or 10s) and set custom starting values. To track multiple categories simultaneously, click "Add Counter" to create separate color-coded tracks for different teams, inventory rows, or classroom behaviors.

Assign custom labels to each counter (such as "Red Team vs. Blue Team", "Laps Completed", or "Event Attendees"). The tool provides optional acoustic click feedback so you can count without looking at the screen. Use the "Reset" button to clear an individual counter or reset all tracks at once.

Why Use ClassPanel's Multi-Track Clicker Counter?

Whether monitoring room attendance at school assemblies, tracking exercise repetitions in physical education, counting laboratory sample occurrences, or tallying classroom house points, mechanical hand tally clickers are easily misplaced. ClassPanel provides a massive, multi-track digital clicker counter that never runs out of batteries.

All active counter tallies and custom labels are saved automatically to your device's local storage. If you accidentally close your browser tab or restart your tablet, your running counts are instantly restored upon re-opening, completely free of charge and tracking-free.
