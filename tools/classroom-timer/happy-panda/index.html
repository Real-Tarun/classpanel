Happy Panda Timer — A Playful Classroom Countdown with Character

The Happy Panda Timer brings personality to your classroom countdown. The panda mascot bounces when the timer is running, gets worried as time runs low, panics in the final seconds — and celebrates with you when it hits zero!

Designed for Non-Distracting Fun

The panda reacts subtly to time passing without pulling students off-task. It's a gentle, motivating visual signal that keeps the classroom atmosphere light and engaging without being a distraction.
