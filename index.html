Frequently Asked Questions for Teachers

Is ClassPanel really completely free?

Yes, ClassPanel is 100% free for educators, schools, and students. There are no paywalls, premium subscriptions, or sign-up requirements. You can bookmark and project any tool instantly.

How do I save my student class rosters?

In tools like the Random Name Picker and Group Generator, open the roster manager, paste your student names, and click "Save Roster". ClassPanel saves them locally in your browser so they are ready every time you open class.

How do I use Fullscreen Projector Mode?

Every tool page features a dark "Projector Mode" button at the top-right. Clicking it activates a distraction-free full-screen display that hides browser tabs and navigation bars. It also requests a wake-lock so your smartboard doesn't go to sleep.

Will the tools work on my interactive smartboard?

Yes! All tools are built with oversized, touch-friendly touch targets tested on Promethean Boards, SMART Boards, ViewSonic ViewBoards, and interactive panels, as well as Chromebooks and iPads.
