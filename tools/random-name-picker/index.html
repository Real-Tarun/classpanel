How to Use the Random Name Picker & Wheel of Names

Paste or type your student roster, raffle tickets, or choice options into the text editor box, with one name per line. The animated wheel automatically subdivides itself into vibrant, equal-sized color wedges corresponding to each entry. Click the center of the wheel or press the big "Spin Wheel" button to launch the physics-based spinning animation with authentic acoustic clicking sounds.

When the wheel decelerates and lands on the winner, a festive celebration modal appears with digital confetti. To ensure every student gets an equal opportunity to participate, enable the "Remove Name Upon Selection" toggle. Each chosen name is safely removed from the active wheel until your entire class has been called, after which you can restore the full roster with a single click.

Why Use ClassPanel's Wheel of Names in Class?

Calling on students manually often leads to unconscious teacher bias or complaints of favoritism from peers. ClassPanel's digital wheel guarantees algorithmic fairness using cryptographically secure random values. Students stay alert and engaged because the spinning visual creates suspense and makes every cold call feel like a fair, impartial game.

Student privacy is strictly protected under FERPA and COPPA guidelines. Your student rosters and class lists are stored exclusively in your local browser sandbox (localStorage) and are never sent to external servers or ad networks. The tool operates smoothly offline on interactive smartboards, tablets, and desktop computers alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save and switch between multiple classroom rosters?

Yes! You can paste student lists and save different rosters (e.g., Period 1, Period 2, Reading Group). Rosters are stored securely in your browser local storage.

Can I remove a student name once they have been picked?

Yes. Enable the "Eliminate Selected" toggle to automatically remove chosen names until every student has had an equal opportunity.

Is student data kept completely private?

Yes. All student rosters are stored solely on your local device. No roster data is ever transmitted or stored on remote servers (FERPA/COPPA safe).
