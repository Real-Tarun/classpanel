Teaching Effective Public Speaking and Time Management

Whether preparing students for Model UN, debate club, or classroom science presentations, keeping within allocated time limits is a crucial life skill.

Traffic Light Meaning

  • Green Light: The speaker has satisfied the minimum required duration for grading.
  • Yellow Light: One minute remaining. The speaker should begin their concluding remarks.
  • Red Light: Time has expired. Conclude speaking immediately.
