Understanding Digital Color Spaces: HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK

Digital design and classroom presentations depend on consistent color rendering across different screens, projectors, and print materials:

  • HEX (Hexadecimal): The 6-character web standard representing red, green, and blue light channels in base-16 notation (e.g. #4F46E5).
  • RGB (Red, Green, Blue): Additive color model used for computer monitors, phone screens, and digital classroom smartboards (values 0–255).
  • HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness): The most human-intuitive color model. Modifying the lightness or saturation percentage lets you generate cohesive tints and shades effortlessly.
  • WCAG Accessibility: The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines require a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal body text and 3:1 for large display titles to ensure students with visual impairments can read clearly.
