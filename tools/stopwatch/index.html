How to Use the Online Stopwatch with Laps

Click the large green "Start" button or press the spacebar on your keyboard to begin timing. The stopwatch records elapsed time down to hundredths of a second (10 milliseconds) in large, high-contrast digital numerals. While the stopwatch is running, click the "Lap" button to capture intermediate split times without interrupting the master clock.

Each recorded lap appears in the structured table below, displaying the lap number, split duration, and cumulative elapsed time. The tool automatically highlights your fastest lap in emerald green and slowest lap in coral red for instant athletic and scientific comparison. Click "Reset" to clear the board for the next trial.

Why Use ClassPanel's Split Lap Stopwatch?

Whether conducting science laboratory experiments (such as timing pendulum swings or chemical reaction rates), pacing physical education sprints, or timing student speed reading drills, precision timekeeping is essential. ClassPanel provides clean, distraction-free millisecond measurement.

The stopwatch runs using high-precision hardware timestamps (performance.now()), preventing the timer drift that afflicts ordinary browser timers. It requires no installation, runs offline as a progressive web app, and saves no tracking cookies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this online stopwatch?

The stopwatch utilizes the browser high-resolution timer (`performance.now()`), accurate to 1 millisecond for scientific experiments and athletic trials.

Can I record unlimited split laps?

Yes, click "Lap" or press the L key to log split benchmarks with automatic identification of your fastest and slowest laps.
